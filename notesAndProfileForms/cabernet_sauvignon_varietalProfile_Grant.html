Cabernet Sauvignon — Varietal Profile

Overview

Cabernet Sauvignon is a full-bodied red grape originating from Bordeaux, France, and now one of the world's most planted varietals. Renowned for its structure, aging potential, and bold flavors, it often shows dark fruit, herbaceous notes, and firm tannins, making it a benchmark for powerful reds.

Core Characteristics

  • Primary Flavors: Blackcurrant, black cherry, cassis, green bell pepper, mint, cedar, graphite, vanilla (from oak)
  • Body: Full
  • Tannin: High
  • Acidity: Medium to high
  • Sweetness: Dry

Aroma & Flavor

Aroma Profile: Intense dark fruits like blackcurrant and plum, with herbal undertones of green pepper or eucalyptus, plus cedar and tobacco from aging.

Flavor Profile: Robust dark berry core, evolving to earthy notes, pencil shavings, and spice; oak-aged versions add vanilla and mocha, with a long, tannic finish.

Origin & Style

Common Regions: Bordeaux (France, often blended); Napa Valley, Sonoma (California); Coonawarra (Australia); Maipo Valley (Chile); Tuscany (Italy, as Super Tuscans)

Winemaking Notes: Thrives in gravelly soils and moderate climates; frequently oak-aged (French for elegance, American for bolder vanilla); single-varietal or blended for complexity and balance.

Pairing & Service

Best With: Grilled steaks, lamb, hearty stews, aged cheeses like cheddar or gouda, dark chocolate desserts

Serve Temperature: 60–65°F

Decant Time: 1–2 hours for young wines to soften tannins

Aging Potential: 10–30+ years for premium examples, developing elegance over time

Vibes / Personality

Commanding, structured, and sophisticated—reliable depth that rewards patience and reveals more with time.

Personal Notes

A powerhouse varietal ideal for building tasting skills; focus on how tannins evolve with air and food.
